WAYPOINT STUDIO — Secure AI & Cybersecurity Studio
Tagline: "Build bold with AI. Arrive safely."

Waypoint Studio is a small, security-first AI studio based in Hamra, Beirut (a short walk from AUB), founded in 2025. We help small and mid-sized businesses, NGOs, and cultural organizations adopt AI without opening new doors to attackers. Our mission is simple: make trustworthy AI accessible to the organizations that need it most but can least afford a breach. We serve clients in Lebanon, across the MENA region, and remotely in Europe, and we work in English, Arabic, and French.

We offer five core services. (1) Secure AI Agent Builds: custom chatbots and AI agents (customer support, lead capture, internal knowledge assistants) built with guardrails against prompt injection and data leakage; packages start at USD 2,500. (2) Breach Rehearsal, our AI red-team audit: we attack your existing LLM application using the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and deliver a plain-language report with fixes; fixed price of USD 1,200 for a single application. (3) Small Business Security Health Check: a two-week review covering phishing exposure, MFA, backups, Wi-Fi, and cloud accounts, for USD 600. (4) Carry-On Security: digital-safety preparation for travelers, journalists, and remote teams, including device hardening and a travel kit; USD 90 per person or USD 400 for group workshops of up to 15 people. (5) Studio Shield: security and responsible-AI support for galleries, bookshops, musicians, and other creatives, protecting their websites, accounts, and intellectual property; quoted per project. We also run "Phish & Chips" lunchtime awareness sessions for teams.

The team is four people. Marwa Deeb (Founder & Principal Security Engineer) holds an ME in Cybersecurity from the American University of Beirut and leads every audit personally. Karim Saab (Lead AI Engineer) builds and evaluates the agents. Lea Khoury (Design & Client Experience Lead, former gallery curator) makes every report and interface readable by non-engineers. Jad Nassar (Training & Awareness Lead) runs the workshops.

What makes Waypoint different: every agent we build ships with its own red-team report — we attack our own work before we hand it over. We explain risk in plain language, never use client data to train models, and design with the care of a studio rather than the coldness of a vendor.
